\section{Release boundaries and next implementation work}\label{sec:conclusion}
The current product provides contract-level financial scope, a graphical owner workspace, source-mode onboarding, MCP integrations, recoverable service procurement and independently graded evidence. The public native-agent case and the controlled adversarial experiments support specific claims about this implementation. They do not justify treating the previous research proposal's entire intended protocol as deployed.

The next engineering priorities are signed installation and updates, persistent local practice recovery, remaining operating-system/wallet usability paths, stronger isolation of session signing and retention, and broader tested merchant/facilitator compatibility. A genuinely independent paid-merchant HTTPS witness and externally managed latest-head retention require additional deployment and trust design. Beacon-bound decision commitments, zero-knowledge execution evidence and ERC-8004 integration require separate specifications and experiments.

The task checklist remains authoritative: the original T0--T11 set retains 97/105 completed subitems, T12 retains 7/8, and G3 remains open in this snapshot. The new document does not change runtime code, authorize mainnet spending or constitute an official submission. Academic programme/year information on the title page is the submitted registration information, not a newly inferred biography.
