T11.03–07 documents/deck/video/reproduced source/execution index and T11.10 candidate privacy check are delivered for this snapshot; all other checklist rows retain their own acceptance requirements. A document snapshot is not the final submission freeze or a third-party audit.

T11.08 has a versioned audit of the current primary materials in `competition/MATERIALS.json`: quantitative claims, native chart values, notes/captions, literal addresses/references and twelve bounded code/evidence claims. It also preserves the deliberately wrong-gas negative control, which the checker rejected. Rc4 adds current-source reproduction; the older rc2 and hosted site retain their recorded versions. Changes to materials require a new audit; T11.09 freeze remains separate.

The dated rules review treats the video as required, while the attachment calls it optional. We will prepare it. The public-testnet acceptance question remains unresolved. Mainnet payments and formal submission require explicit user authorization. A complete testnet product does not automatically close the mainnet part of T4 or the related gate.

The presentation and video should use this order: task and budget → readable owner approval → discover/check a service → registered payment → actual settlement → service/result checks → evidence verification → revoke and reject → recover the original order. Screens from design/mandate-preview are clearly simulated and cannot stand in for these executed steps.

The final package must distinguish original Echo work, the M2 security repair, new Mandate behavior and the scientific questions from the previous proposal. Tests support only their recorded scope; no universal security guarantee, third-party audit, live service purchase or installed MCP integration should be claimed without its own evidence.
